## Why a blog?

Building projects is one thing. Explaining *why* you built them — and what you learned along the way — is another.

This blog is where I'll share the thinking behind the ecosystem: architecture decisions, research notes, and the occasional post-mortem on things that didn't work.

## What to expect

- **Technical deep-dives** into infrastructure and tooling
- **Research notes** from ongoing papers
- **Building in public** — honest updates on what's shipping and what's stuck

Stay tuned.
